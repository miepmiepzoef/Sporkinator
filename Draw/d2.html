How Flow works

Two menus

Brush — how each individual stroke looks and multiplies as you draw: color, style, strand count, size, drawing symmetry.

Loop — global settings for the whole animation: motion, life, timing, screen tiling, background.

Motion vs Life — two independent controls

Motion Phase controls how strokes move. Life Stagger controls how they fade.

  • Phase 0% — all strokes move together as one coordinated unit
  • Phase 100% — each stroke at a different point in the motion cycle (scattered)
  • Stagger 0% — all strokes fade together (pulsing)
  • Stagger 100% — strokes fade in the sequence they were drawn (flowing)

Try Phase 0% + Stagger 60% for the coordinated motion with a smooth handoff between drawings.

Cycles

Motion cycles — how many full motion cycles per loop. 1× = calm, 2×/3× = faster.

Life cycles — how many fade transitions per loop. More cycles = more handoffs.

Only whole numbers keep the loop seamless.

Rotate amount

Rotate at 45° with 8-fold symmetry = exactly one visual rotation per loop — the calmest setting. Drop to 20° for a subtle drift; raise to 360° for a full spin.

Why it loops perfectly

Every frame is calculated from the clock, not from the previous frame. At time t and time t + loopLength, every stroke has the same phase — so the picture is identical.

Quality: Full / Single

These only affect what's drawn on screen — never the stored strokes, never the export.

  • Full — everything: symmetry + screen tiling
  • Single — one centered copy of the full symmetric pattern, no tiling

The amber number on the quality button shows paths per frame when heavy.

Pause / Undo / Clear

Pause freezes the clock — you can still draw, and strokes bloom in on resume. Unlimited undo/redo. Clear is undoable too.

Export

Video is rendered offline at full quality — expect 1–3 minutes for 4K. Keep the tab open during rendering. Edges are sharp at any resolution because frames are rendered from stored points.
