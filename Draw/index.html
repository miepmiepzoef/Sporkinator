How Flow works (v5)

Two menus

Brush — how each stroke looks and multiplies as you draw. Loop — global animation, tiling, and background.

Randomize

Press 🎲 Random for an instant demo loop. It picks a palette, symmetry, motion, life mode, and loop length, then generates a handful of strokes. Undo brings your old drawing back.

Motion vs Life

  • Phase 0% — strokes move together
  • Phase 100% — scattered
  • Stagger 0% — fade together
  • Stagger 100% — fade in draw order

Drawing assist

  • Stabilize — smooths jitter
  • Snap to center — near-center points snap exactly
