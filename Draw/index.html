How Flow works

The three layers

Every stroke is drawn through three independent filters:

  • Brush — what it looks like: solid, dotted, dashed, tapered, glow, strand count.
  • Motion — how it moves over its life. Global setting.
  • Life — how it fades, grows, scatters, or blinks over its life. Global setting.

Brush settings are snapshotted per stroke when you draw. Motion, life, symmetry, multiplication, loop length and background are global and change the whole scene at once.

Why it loops perfectly

Every frame is calculated from the clock, not from the previous frame. A stroke's age is (now − birth) mod loopLength. At time t and time t + loopLength, every stroke has the same age, so the picture is identical. No drifting, no one-shot recordings.

Quality: Full / Draft / Solo

These only affect what's drawn on screen — never the stored strokes, never the export.

  • Full — everything.
  • Draft — 1 strand, no gradient, no neon. Symmetry and multiplication still show.
  • Solo — just your raw drawing: 1 strand, 1 symmetry copy, plain color.

Draw heavy scenes in Solo at full speed, then tap to Full to reveal the whole pattern instantly. The amber number on the button shows how many paths are being drawn per frame.

Pause

Pause freezes the clock. You can still draw while paused — the strokes are stored but invisible until you resume, at which point they all bloom in together. Handy for building a scene deliberately.

Export

The video is rendered offline, frame by frame, at full quality — independent of your current quality setting. Expect 1–3 minutes for 4K. Keep the tab open during rendering.

Frames are rendered from the stored points, so edges stay sharp at any resolution.
